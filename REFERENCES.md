# References

## Dragon Ball Z M.U.G.E.N Resources
- [Anderson Kenya MUGEN Community - Dragon Ball Z Category](https://www.andersonkenya1.net/files/category/38-dragon-ball-z/): Community warehouse category with DBZ MUGEN characters, edits, and patches.
- [Team Z2 Official Community Releases](https://network.mugenguild.com/balthazar/): Hyper Dragon Ball Z project by Balthazar, Cybaster, and Team Z2.
- [IDOTTTTT/Anime-Mugen](https://github.com/IDOTTTTT/Anime-Mugen): Open repository hosting Team Z2 character packages (SSJ Goku Z2, C18 Z2, Buu Z2, Gohan Z2, Gotenks Z2, TenShinHan Z2, Androids 13/16/17/19, Cell, Frieza, Piccolo, Songoku).
- [DBZ-Mugen Organization](https://github.com/DBZ-Mugen): MUGEN repositories for Bardock, Raditz, and Frieza.
- [BillsLasa MUGEN Fighters](https://github.com/BillsLasa): 2D fighters for Bills (Beerus) and Kid Goku for M.U.G.E.N.
## Naruto & Bleach M.U.G.E.N Resources
- [Anderson Kenya MUGEN Community - Bleach Category](https://www.andersonkenya1.net/files/category/25-bleach/): AK1 Community warehouse category 25 for Bleach fighters, JUS edits, TYBW releases, and soundpacks.
- [Meiloorun/mugen-ichigo-tybw-jus](https://github.com/Meiloorun/mugen-ichigo-tybw-jus): Ichigo Kurosaki (Thousand Year Blood War) True Shikai and Bankai for M.U.G.E.N.
- [Bleach MUGEN Community Releases](https://www.mediafire.com): Verified character packages for Yamamoto Genryusai (Joey Jostar), Hirako Shinji (Lucaszin), Soi Fon (Santoryu/Darkblood), Unohana TYBW (Mounir), Aizen Sosuke TYBW (Salah/Sianza), Toshiro Hitsugaya Adult (Mounir), and Renji Abarai JUS (Akashi Mugen/Arcutomory).
- [BillsLasa/2D-Naruto-Hokage-Fighter---M.U.G.E.N.](https://github.com/BillsLasa/2D-Naruto-Hokage-Fighter---M.U.G.E.N.): Repositório de Naruto (Nanadaime Hokage) em formato M.U.G.E.N por Bills Dios.
- [BillsLasa/2D-Naruto-The-Last-Fighter---M.U.G.E.N.](https://github.com/BillsLasa/2D-Naruto-The-Last-Fighter---M.U.G.E.N.): Repositório de Naruto (The Last) em formato M.U.G.E.N por Bills Dios.
- [Internet Archive - Naruto Battle Arena (2003)](https://archive.org/details/Naruto-Battle-Arena-w2003-06-14b): Preservação histórica dos lutadores clássicos de Naruto (Sasuke, Kakashi, Itachi, Gaara, Rock Lee, Minato, Sakura) e cenários da Vila da Folha.
- [Internet Archive - Bleach MUGEN](https://archive.org/details/bleach-mugen): Coleção preservada de personagens clássicos de Bleach (Ichigo, Bankai Ichigo, Rukia, Renji, Aizen, Byakuya, Hitsugaya, Grimmjow, Ulquiorra).
- [Internet Archive - Dragonball GT MUGEN Ssj4gogeta](https://archive.org/details/ssj4gogeta): Preservação de personagens de Dragon Ball GT (Goku SSJ GT, Vegeta GT, Piccolo) e cenários clássicos (Sala do Tempo, Deserto, Montanhas).
