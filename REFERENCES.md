# References

## Dragon Ball Z M.U.G.E.N Resources
- [Anderson Kenya MUGEN Community - Dragon Ball Z Category](https://www.andersonkenya1.net/files/category/38-dragon-ball-z/): Community warehouse category with DBZ MUGEN characters, edits, and patches.
- [Team Z2 Official Community Releases](https://network.mugenguild.com/balthazar/): Hyper Dragon Ball Z project by Balthazar, Cybaster, and Team Z2.
- [IDOTTTTT/Anime-Mugen](https://github.com/IDOTTTTT/Anime-Mugen): Open repository hosting Team Z2 character packages (SSJ Goku Z2, C18 Z2, Buu Z2, Gohan Z2, Gotenks Z2, TenShinHan Z2, Androids 13/16/17/19, Cell, Frieza, Piccolo, Songoku).
- [DBZ-Mugen Organization](https://github.com/DBZ-Mugen): MUGEN repositories for Bardock, Raditz, and Frieza.
- [BillsLasa MUGEN Fighters](https://github.com/BillsLasa): 2D fighters for Bills (Beerus) and Kid Goku for M.U.G.E.N.

- [BillsLasa/2D-Naruto-Hokage-Fighter---M.U.G.E.N.](https://github.com/BillsLasa/2D-Naruto-Hokage-Fighter---M.U.G.E.N.): Repositório de Naruto (Nanadaime Hokage) em formato M.U.G.E.N por Bills Dios.
- [BillsLasa/2D-Naruto-The-Last-Fighter---M.U.G.E.N.](https://github.com/BillsLasa/2D-Naruto-The-Last-Fighter---M.U.G.E.N.): Repositório de Naruto (The Last) em formato M.U.G.E.N por Bills Dios.

- [Internet Archive - Dragonball GT MUGEN Ssj4gogeta](https://archive.org/details/ssj4gogeta): Preservação de personagens de Dragon Ball GT (Goku SSJ GT, Vegeta GT, Piccolo, Gogeta SSJ4, Goku SSJ4, Vegeta SSJ4) e cenários clássicos.
- [Internet Archive - Warusaki3 MUGEN Characters](https://archive.org/details/warusaki3-mugen-characters): Conversões CPS3 de alta fidelidade de JoJo's Bizarre Adventure (Jotaro Kujo, Dio Brando).

## Tooling
- [potsmugen/mugen-char-linter](https://github.com/potsmugen/mugen-char-linter) (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 PotS): rule set behind `ume_engine::lint` (duplicate parameters, closed-enum values, `persistent` in negative states, `[State]`/`[Statedef]` number mismatch, garbage lines, duplicate `.cmd` keys, duplicate and empty `.air` actions). Rules reimplemented in Rust, no code copied.

## Ultimate MUGEN Engine
- [Ultimate MUGEN Engine](https://github.com/juninmd/ultimate-mugen): 2D fighting game engine in Rust with full MUGEN and UMF compatibility.

## World Wonders Stages
- [New7Wonders of the World](https://world.new7wonders.com/wonders/): Official initiative and documentation of the 7 Modern Wonders of the World (Great Wall of China, Petra, Christ the Redeemer, Machu Picchu, Chichén Itzá, Roman Colosseum, Taj Mahal).
- [Seven Wonders of the Ancient World - Encyclopedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Seven-Wonders-of-the-Ancient-World): Classical historical reference for the 7 Ancient Wonders (Great Pyramid of Giza, Hanging Gardens of Babylon, Temple of Artemis, Statue of Zeus at Olympia, Mausoleum at Halicarnassus, Colossus of Rhodes, Lighthouse of Alexandria).
