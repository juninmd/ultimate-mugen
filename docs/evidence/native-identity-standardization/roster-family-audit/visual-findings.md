## Status and evidence limits

- The engine resolver currently reports zero uncovered frame requirements across all 21 packs. It resolves 6,117 frames: 4,738 exact sprite files, 1,051 shared-pose resolutions, and 328 procedural effects.
- A fresh geometry audit covered all 2,171 combat PNGs: every canvas is 400×400. It found 16 frame anchors just beyond the canvas and 10 frames without explicit geometry metadata; these have now been clamped or filled from neighboring poses/pack geometry. The follow-up audit reports zero canvas or metadata anomalies. It also records 290 sprites whose opaque bounds touch a canvas edge; those need pose-by-pose clipping review and are not automatically treated as defects.
- The pixel-edge/palette screen covers all 2,171 PNGs: the maximum is 47 opaque colors per frame and there are no partially transparent pixels. Of the 290 edge-touching frames, 286 touch the bottom baseline; 69 touch a side or the top. Those positions were screened on `edge-touch-review.png`; any visible clipping still requires native-size adjudication.
- A separate transformation-family sheet uses the engine resolver's actual sources for `powerUp`. All 21 packs resolve three distinct body images; the 16 playable fighters' forms show no obvious identity switch at contact-sheet scale. The initial suspicion that Kaito lacked power-up frames was a filename-assumption error: it uses exact `powerup0.png`–`powerup2.png`. All 16 playable fighters now have stable post-transformation runtime captures; four contact sheets index them in `runtime-transform-audit/awakened-runtime-page-1.png` through `awakened-runtime-page-4.png`. Native-size identity comparisons for all 21 powerUp families are in `transformation-native-review/`; individual frame-cycle runtime capture remains open.
- The confirmed-family backlog has 282 hash-pinned entries: 87 confirmed inconsistencies, 139 identity-consistent files, 56 style-review-pending files and no remaining native-size candidates. The separate correction queue has 374 exact animated files: 221 confirmed style/family mismatches, 36 Akio outfit-family mismatches, 70 Kaname outfit-family mismatches, 45 style candidates and two wrong-family Suzume files.
- These resolver counts prove which image/effect each declared frame reaches. They do not prove character identity, pixel-art finish, consistent scale, or smooth motion.
- The corrected Bruno, Garan and Lian files have before/after evidence in this folder. Stable runtime captures now cover the transformed idle state for all 16 playable fighters. Every screenshot shows the expected fighter identity and form palette after the transformation effect, with no obvious old or cross-character sprite. This validates the current transformed state, not every transition-animation frame or attack in every form. The remaining Garan `hop` mismatch is confirmed in the current contact sheet and source PNG. Image generation is currently unavailable: the image-generation route reports its usage limit reached, and the local Grok CLI has no authenticated account; the prior routed attempt timed out and produced no candidate.
- The in-repo rig renderer was piloted as a local replacement source for Garan's `hop`. Its output retained the disputed silhouette and did not meet the authored sprite pack's visual standard, so the candidate was rejected and the original restored. The pilot also exposed that the generator could rewrite missing inline poses across the whole roster; 44 unreviewed outputs were discarded. `generate_missing_sprites --character=<id>` now scopes future pilots, and a dry run confirmed the Garan-only scope.
- The complete visual checklist is `family-review-checklist.json`; all entries currently have `visualReview: pending` until the actual frame sequence has been inspected.
- Do not describe the full roster as visually standardized until every family has a documented visual disposition, confirmed defects are corrected, and affected families are reviewed in runtime.
