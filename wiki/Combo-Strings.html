Combo strings

Every native fighter (the 16 roster fighters and the 5 Story NPCs) has a signature three-part string on top of the shared gatling rules. Moves and animations are data only: each string_* animation is composed from the fighter's own jab, kick and strong frames (same poses, same hit parts, so sword users swing the weapon and brawlers use fists) with a per-fighter speed, hit count and recovery.

InputMove idRole
B+xstring_aOpener. Also reachable by cancelling jab on contact
B+astring_bMiddle link. Cancel from string_a on contact
B+ystring_cFinisher. Launch, wall bounce or ground bounce depending on the fighter; heavy fighters get one hit of armor

string_a and string_b cancel into string_c and into the fighter's two signature specials on contact, so every fighter has its own route. Examples:

Akio, Jin, Suzume, Phantom, RazorRushdown: three to four fast light hits, long advancestring_a > string_b > string_c > starburst/chakram/volley
Bruno, Brute, Colossus, ValdricHeavy: two slow hits, wall bounce finisher with armorstring_a > string_c > quake/ridgeblast/royallance
Kaname, Shion, Marlow, OboroBlade: weapon strikes, ice/water element where it fitsstring_a > string_b > frost arc/crescent/riptide
Garan, Sylene, Kaito, Volta, SpitfireElemental: every hit carries fire, ice, wind or lightning stunstring_b > string_c > element special
Garan, Lian, Jin, Suzume, Phantom, RazorGround-bounce finisher for a follow-up jugglestring_c

Validated by cargo test --release -p ume-engine --test native_combo_routes (every declared cancel chains on contact), native_roster_lab and native_hitbox_audit.

Same-button mini combos

Pressing the same attack button again after a hit chains into the next move, with no extra direction needed. Each chain move is a normal with a rekka gate (after, window, contact: true), so it only fires from its source on contact and never from neutral. The chain animations reuse the fighter's existing sprites (same pixel art) in new orders and faster timings.

PressChain
xjab > jab2 > jab3 (finisher with small pushback, cancels into the two signature specials)
alkick > lkick2 > lkick3
ystrong > strong2
crouch xcjab > cjab2
air xajab > ajab2

Fighter speed (> 1 shortens every timed frame) and forward walk/run velocity were raised roster-wide; the final boss is scaled with it so it stays faster. Back-walk speeds are unchanged. Validated by cargo test --release -p ume-engine --test native_same_button_chains.

Projectile variants

Every roster fighter has a slow heavy and a fast low variant of its signature projectile, reusing the same effects: ~D, DB, B, y (slow, two hits, long range) and ~D, DB, B, x (fast, low, hits crouching foes). Fighters whose input is already taken use the first free of ~D, DF, F, y and ~D, DF, F, a.
