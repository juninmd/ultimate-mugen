Signature Meter

Português (BR)

Each native fighter can declare a signature block under passives in its JSON. It is a per-fighter meter that changes how the fighter plays, on top of rage, stacks and enrage time.

"passives": {
  "signature": {
    "name": "Storm",
    "max": 100,
    "gainHit": 6,
    "gainSpecial": 12,
    "gainHurt": 3,
    "decayDelaySeconds": 2.5,
    "decayPerSecond": 14,
    "drainOnSuper": true,
    "riskPerSecond": 0,
    "tiers": [
      { "at": 34, "attack": 1.08, "speed": 1.04 },
      { "at": 100, "attack": 1.32, "speed": 1.12, "defence": 0.9 }
    ]
  }
}
KeyMeaning
maxMeter size, 1 to 200.
gainHit, gainSpecial, gainHurtGain when a move connects, a special or super starts, and when hit.
decayDelaySeconds, decayPerSecondIdle time before the meter drains, and the drain rate.
drainOnSuperSpends the meter when a super ends, so the super benefits from it.
riskPerSecondLife lost per second while the meter is full. Never kills.
tiersUp to four steps. The highest reached applies attack (0.5 to 2), defence (0.5 to 1.5) and speed (0.8 to 1.25).

Attack still goes through the global cap in Passives. The meter resets each round and shows as a strip under the life bar.

Current fighters: Kaito (Storm, builds and speeds him up) and Volta (Overload, strong at full meter but costs life).

More fighters: Suzume (Lotus, builds from specials and speeds her up), Marlow (Tide, gains from being hit as well and adds defence), Jin (Wind Chain, builds fast and decays fast for speed) and Sylene (Ice Layers, mostly gained by taking hits, adds defence).
